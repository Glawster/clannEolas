# PER-002: David

> Fictional persona. David is not a real person and these details are not
> household data.

## Situation

David is 72 and lives alone. His daughter lives 300 miles away. He is managing
his own affairs and wants to make things easier if illness or an emergency
means she needs to help from a distance.

David is comfortable with paper and basic digital documents but does not want
his plans tied to an online service. He worries that a large form will be hard
to finish and that detailed copies of financial or identity records could be
unsafe.

## Needs

- A manageable process he can complete a section at a time.
- Clear guidance about what to record and what only to reference.
- Legible, print-friendly pages and accessible language.
- A review checklist that keeps contacts and instructions current.
- A safe way to tell his daughter where the private handbook is held.

## A good outcome

David maintains a concise handbook in a format he controls, and his daughter
knows that it exists, where authorised access instructions are kept and when it
was last reviewed.
