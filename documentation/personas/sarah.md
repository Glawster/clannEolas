# PER-001: Sarah

> Fictional persona. Sarah is not a real person and these details are not
> household data.

## Situation

Sarah is 38, married and has two children. Her partner dies unexpectedly.
While grieving and supporting the children, she must work out which people and
organisations to contact, what needs urgent attention and where important
documents can be found.

Her partner handled several household tasks and online accounts. Sarah does not
need a catalogue of everything they owned; she needs calm, prioritised guidance
that does not expose passwords or force her to learn a new application.

## Needs

- A short emergency starting point with clear next actions.
- Trusted contacts and safe references to important records.
- Explanations in plain language without assuming legal or financial expertise.
- Family routines and care information alongside formal administration.
- A paper or offline copy that works even when account access is uncertain.

## A good outcome

Sarah can identify urgent responsibilities, find the appropriate supporting
information and ask the right people for help without searching through unsafe
or duplicated private details.
