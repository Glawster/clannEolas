## Jurisdiction boundary

An England-and-Wales property and financial affairs Lasting Power of Attorney
(LPA) is not a generic label for all UK authority. Scotland and Northern Ireland
have different instruments, terminology, registration and rules. An ordinary
power, enduring/continuing power, deputyship, guardianship, appointeeship,
third-party mandate and nominated contact also have different scopes and
activation conditions.

Eolas does not determine mental capacity or legal authority. A diagnosis, age,
family relationship, joint account, possession of a card or history of informal
help is not by itself proof that a person may transact for someone else.

## Preparation and registration

- Identify the exact authority, jurisdiction, registration, start/end,
  activation condition, restrictions and how multiple representatives must act.
- Preserve the person's involvement, wishes and supported decision-making as
  far as possible.
- Record evidence by safe reference and classify copies appropriately.
- Register authority separately with each institution and record its reference,
  requested identity/address evidence, accepted scope and issued access method.
- Use a representative login, card or mandate issued by the institution. Never
  use the account holder's login, PIN or security answers.
- Review joint accounts and essential continuity dependencies with each
  institution rather than assuming unrestricted operation.

## Acting and records

- Act only within the authority, restrictions and applicable duties.
- Keep the person's money separate except where a legitimate pre-existing joint
  arrangement requires specific treatment.
- Preserve decisions, receipts, conflicts, gifts, loans, unusual transactions
  and the evidence supporting significant action.
- Respect confidentiality and share only the information required for the task.
- Record provider refusals or barriers and use the provider's complaint/support
  route where appropriate.
- Re-review authority after a change in capacity, representative, address,
  institution, relationship or legal status.

## Temporary assistance

When a person retains capacity but needs short-term help, prefer a provider-
supported third-party mandate, accessible service or applicable ordinary power
with a clear scope and end date. Credential sharing is not a continuity plan.
Revoke temporary access and review activity when assistance ends.
