# Clann Eolas

A private, practical record of the information a connected group of people may
need when they cannot manage things themselves.

The root entity is a **Clann**. A Clann contains people whose lives and
responsibilities are connected, even when they live in different households.
Household membership records where people live without defining whether or how
they are related.
