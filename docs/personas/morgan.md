# P-004: Morgan

> Fictional persona. Morgan is not a real person and these details are not
> household data.

## Situation

Morgan is 29 and lives with their partner in a rented home. They have seen
family planning products that encourage people to place credentials, identity
details and financial information in one file. They want to prepare for an
emergency but are cautious about privacy and vendor lock-in.

Morgan is willing to use open-source templates and may contribute improvements,
provided it is obvious which material is safe to publish and which belongs only
in a private household copy.

## Needs

- A clear boundary between public templates and private household data.
- Safe examples that demonstrate useful detail without realistic identifiers.
- Explicit warnings against storing passwords, PINs and recovery codes.
- Open formats that can be kept locally, printed or moved between tools.
- Transparent requirements and ADRs explaining why safety choices were made.

## A good outcome

Morgan can prepare a useful handbook without creating an avoidable store of
secrets and can contribute generic improvements without publishing private
household information.
