# PER-003: Amina

> Fictional persona. Amina is not a real person and these details are not
> household data.

## Situation

Amina is 45 and helps coordinate care for her mother after a sudden loss of
capacity. Other relatives also help, but knowledge about medication, routines,
appointments, home maintenance and legal authority is scattered between them.

Amina needs to distinguish immediate care information from records that require
professional interpretation. She may be reading on a phone at one moment and
using a printed page with a family member at another.

## Needs

- One reliable home for each piece of guidance, without conflicting copies.
- Clear separation between facts, wishes, tasks and professional advice.
- Accessible layouts that work across paper and simple digital formats.
- References to protected health and legal records without unnecessary copies.
- A way to identify gaps and follow-up actions during review.

## A good outcome

Amina and authorised relatives can coordinate practical care from consistent,
current instructions while recognising when they need a clinician, solicitor
or other qualified professional.
