# 004: Fictional example household

Legacy ID: `HB-004`  
Priority: high  
Owner: project maintainers

## Status

ToDo

## Outcome

As a first-time handbook owner, I need a clearly fictional example so that I
can understand safe, proportionate completion without copying risky patterns.

## Context

The example must demonstrate references to protected material rather than
reproducing it, remain separate from blank handbook content and represent varied
family, care, accessibility and practical needs.

## Scope

- A coherent fictional household across representative core chapters.
- Conspicuous labels and safe placeholders for sensitive information.

## Out of scope

- Real or lightly anonymised household data.
- Credentials, government identifiers, account numbers or precise addresses.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A privacy review confirms all data is fictional, non-identifying and unusable for account or identity access.
2. The example demonstrates safe completion of at least one prompt from every agreed core chapter.
3. Removing the example does not remove instructions needed to use the blank handbook.
